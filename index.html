How to play

  • Move ← → or A / D · Jump Space, ↑, W or Z (hold for higher)
  • Run hold Shift or X · with the Ember power it also throws fireballs
  • Ground-pound press ↓ in mid-air to smash bricks and stun enemies
  • Stomp enemies; chain stomps without touching the ground for bigger points and a 1UP
  • Hit sparkle blocks for coins and power-ups: Sun Berry, Ember Bloom, Sky Feather (double-jump & glide), Star Shard
  • Find the 3 star medals in every stage and beat the par time to earn all 3 stars
  • Spend coins in the Shop, claim Achievements and your Daily Reward
